# Exhaust the Design Space

When a decision has no precedent in the codebase, build two or three competing prototypes and compare them side by side before you commit to one. The first idea that works is rarely the best one, and a throwaway prototype costs less than a rewrite after the design has callers.

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.
